Cinematic · getting started

Lights down, world up

Cinematic fades your UI between fights, adds letterbox bars and a color tint, and takes the camera for flights, campfires, quest givers and more. Here's how to install it and get going in a couple of minutes.

Step one

Install it

  1. Get it from CurseForge

    In the CurseForge app, pick the game version you play, search for Cinematic and install it. You can also download it from CurseForge's website and unzip the Cinematic folder into the game's Interface › AddOns folder for that version.

  2. Any version of the game

    One download works everywhere, from retail to the Classic versions. It's made first and foremost for WoW Forever, so that's where it's tested most. Settings that only apply to one game show up only there, and each game keeps its own settings. See WoW Forever and retail for what's on which.

  3. Log in

    Make sure Cinematic is ticked in the AddOns list on the character screen, then enter the world.

Step two

The welcome window

The first time you log in, a window asks: Immerse yourself in the world. How much do you want? Nothing runs until you pick an answer, and picking one tries it straight away, so you can see it in the game before you close the window.

Subtle

A softer look. A color tint, a vignette and weather over your normal UI. No camera moves on its own and nothing hides.

Balanced

Clears your screen between fights. Your UI fades away for the letterbox and tint, and comes straight back for a fight. The camera takes over now and then.

Cinema

Just the world, like a film. The game's UI stays away even in fights, and Cinematic shows what you need its own way. Every camera.

The Profiles guide goes through each level in detail.

Next
Goes on to Customize, where you change one part of your level at a time: layout, interface, action bars, camera, look, and sound and chat. Each change shows straight away, and your first change starts a profile of your own called My setup.
Settings
Closes the window and opens Cinematic's settings, at the Profiles page (or, from Customize, the page for the part you're on).
Done
Closes the window and keeps the level you picked. Before you've picked one it says Decide later: Cinematic stays off, and the window comes back at your next login.
Turn off
Turns Cinematic off (the window stays open, so you can still pick a level). It stays off until you pick a level or press Turn on.

Closing the window with the X or Escape keeps what you picked, like Done. To open it again later, type /cine welcome.

Day to day

Turning CineMode on and off

CineMode is Cinematic at work: the fading, the letterbox and the cameras. Once you've picked a level, switch it on or off any of these ways:

  • Type /cine in the chat box. It flips CineMode on or off. /cine on and /cine off set it one way.
  • Set a key for Toggle CineMode on the Keybinds page (or under Key Bindings › AddOns).
  • Tick or untick Enable Cinematic on Cinematic's main settings page or in the minimap button's menu.

If you haven't picked a level yet, /cine opens the welcome window instead, since there's nothing to turn on.

While CineMode is on, fights, casting and opening windows like the spellbook bring your UI back, and pointing at a faded frame shows it. Typing just shows the chat. You can also have it turn itself off in places where you'd rather have the full UI, such as fights, cities, inns or dungeons, from Turn off in on the main settings page.

Want the look without the fading? Untick Start in CineMode on the main settings page. The UI then stays as it is and the camera's left to you, with just the visual effects over it, as in the Subtle level.

Quick switches

The minimap button

Cinematic puts a spyglass button on the edge of your minimap. Point at it to see whether Cinematic is on.

Left-click
A quick menu: Enable Cinematic, Show minimap button, Turn off in combat, music on or off, the tint's strength, and Open settings. While a vision (Heal, Quest, Duel, Roll) is in use, the button glows and the menu lists it with a link to its page.
Right-click
Opens Cinematic's settings.
Drag
Moves the button around the minimap's edge.

The button fades with the minimap in CineMode. If you hide it, bring it back with /cine button or the Show minimap button option on the main settings page.

Commands

A few to start with

Type these in the chat box. /cinematic works too. Just /cine help (or anything it doesn't know) lists them all in chat.

/cineTurns CineMode on or off
/cine welcomeOpens the welcome window, to pick a level or Customize
/cine configOpens Cinematic's settings
/cine buttonShows or hides the minimap button

Every command is on the Commands page.

Going further

Where the settings are

Open the game's Options, then AddOns › Cinematic (or type /cine config). The main page has a search box to find any setting, and each page has a Reset page button that puts just that page back. The Settings map lists every page in one line each.

Something looks wrong. Where do I start?
See Troubleshooting. /cine panic stops Cinematic for the session and puts your UI and game settings back.
Can I use it in another language?
Yes. Pick one from Language on the main settings page: English, Deutsch, Español, Français, Italiano, Português, Русский, 한국어, 简体中文 or 繁體中文. It changes after a reload.
Will it change my game settings?
Your own sound and game settings are saved first and always put back, even after a crash.
