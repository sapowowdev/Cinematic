World of Warcraft addon

Cinematic Guidebook

Cinematic fades your UI away between fights, slides in a letterbox and a tint that follows the time of day, and takes the camera for flights, campfires, quest givers, going AFK and more. These pages explain how its parts work.

About these pages

Cinematic runs on WoW Forever and retail. Where something differs between them, the page lets you pick yours.

More guides are added as features grow. The addon's code is on GitHub.
