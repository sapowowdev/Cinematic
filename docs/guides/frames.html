Cinematic · frames

Every frame, when you need it

In CineMode the game's frames fade away. You decide which come back, and when: in fights, with a target, in a dungeon, or all the time. A handful of pages then add the clever parts, like a player frame that stays while you recover.

The main page

Standard Frames: a row per frame

The Standard Frames page, in the UI Manager, lists the game's own frames, one row each. In CineMode everything on it is hidden unless its row says otherwise. Each row reads left to right:

  • AlwaysShows the frame all through CineMode. The other columns then have nothing to add.
  • CombatA dropdown: In combat, Calm Timer (after a fight, until it runs out), Out of combat, or In a camera mode (flights, AFK, cozy and the rest).
  • TargetA dropdown: Enemy targeted or Friend targeted, out of fights (friends, NPCs and dead enemies count as friends here).
  • LocationA dropdown of places: BGs & Arenas, Open World (inns included), Cities, Instances (dungeons, scenarios and raids), and Grouped (in a party or raid, wherever you are).
  • RolesTick roles to show the frame only while you're one of them: your specialization's, or the one picked in the group finder. None ticked means every role.
  • EditTurns the row into a rule, for when the dropdowns aren't enough. A dot shows whether the row is on right now.

Anything ticked in the three dropdowns shows the frame; they join with OR. Frames fade in and out at the fade times on the CineMode page. Hovering a faded frame always brings it up, along with its mouseover group.

Rules

When a row needs more: Edit

Press Edit on a row to open the rule editor. Its buttons add words in groups: Logic (AND, OR, NOT and brackets), Combat, Target (also Anything targeted, by kind of unit: Enemy Players, Hostile NPCs, Friendly NPCs and the rest, and Plate off screen: your target's nameplate isn't on screen, as when it's behind the camera or too far away, and Near: your target within about 11 yards, or 28 yards with Near (28 yards)),Location (Inns, Dungeons and Raids apart too), You (Mounted, PvP flagged, Grouped) and Role. So a row can say "In combat AND (Open World OR BGs & Arenas)", "NOT Mounted", or, on the Target row, "Plate off screen OR Near" to bring the target frame up only while you can't see your target's nameplate, or it's up close, where the game pins its nameplate to the top of the screen.

Each button shows a green dot where it's true right now. Press Done to keep the rule. A rule that's just the dropdowns' words joined by OR goes back into them; anything more stays as the row's rule, shown in its place. Reset to grid drops the rule and brings the old ticks back.

Switches at the top

  • In combat rows stay for the Calm Timer: frames ticked In combat stay up after a fight until the Calm Timer (CineMode page) runs out. Off, they fade as soon as the fight ends.
  • Cast fade into combat: out of combat, casting a spell at an enemy brings up the In combat frames as if the fight had started. They're fully up as the spell lands.
  • Show the pet frame when your pet isn't happy (hunters on Classic clients): keeps your pet frame up while its happiness isn't green, with Hide in camera modes under it.
The other pages

Pages that keep a frame up for a reason

Standard Frames says when a frame may show. These pages add why: they bring a frame up because something happened. All but Personal Resources have their own Enable page switch; off, the frame just follows its Standard Frames row (on Unit Frames, every part shows as the game has it).

Unit Frames

Which parts of the unit frames show, CineMode or not, to keep just the bars if you like. Click a frame's name to open its grid: your own frame, your pet's, and the target, focus and target of target by who they show (you, enemy players, hostile NPCs, neutral players, neutral NPCs, friendly players, friendly NPCs).

  • Parts: portrait, name, level, frame art (the portrait ring, name box and bar borders, which the game draws as one picture), PvP icon, icons (rest Zzz, leader's crown, role, quest and raid marker), auras, hit text, threat and cast bar.
  • Same as target: the focus and target of target follow the target's grid until you untick it.
Personal Resources

Your player frame, pet frame and personal resource display.

  • After a fight: keep your player frame, and separately your resource display, up until your health and power are full. Rage and runic power can count too, until they've drained to 0. Your pet frame can stay until its health is full.
  • Low mana: below the share you pick, your resource bar shows in a fight or not, while drinking or saving mana for the pull. It can bring your player frame too.
  • At the start of a fight: the resource bar, health bar and player frame can each wait until something changes. Go in with full mana and the bar comes up with your first cast; the health bar with the first hit or heal you take.
Cooldown Manager

The game's Cooldown Manager (Enhanced Cooldown Manager's bars too). Hovering it always shows it.

  • Stay up while a cooldown is recharging, for the Essential and Utility bars apart, optionally only after combat (within the Calm Timer).
  • Spell icons: for each spell, when its icon shows. Always as the game has it; When Ready fades it while it recharges and brings it back once ready, or a few seconds early (Show Early), and can wait for the Resources to cast it; While recharging; or When it procs, only while the game makes it glow.
  • Close the gaps (centered): the shown icons sit together, centered, instead of faded ones leaving holes. On for Utility to start with.
Swing timer
Classic clients
  • Only show the weapons you're using: main and off-hand while you auto-attack, ranged while you shoot, each fading a while after it's done.
  • Hide the off-hand bar, dual wielding or not.
  • Ranged bar on top, set per class (ticked for hunters, mages, priests and warlocks to start with), so one profile suits all your characters.
Buffs/debuffs
  • Show buffs when you gain or refresh one, for a few seconds; optionally only after combat, and not for ones you cast on yourself.
  • Show debuffs when you gain or refresh one, in fights too, and keep them up until the debuff is gone.
  • Long ones (Power Word: Fortitude, Deserter) can be left out by length, and any buff or debuff by name or spell ID. Never on flights.
Minimap
  • Reveal: the faded minimap comes up when ore, herbs or treasure show on it, a finished quest's turn-in or a quest area shows, or another named icon appears. It can ping new nodes for you (your group sees the ping). Not on flight paths.
  • Pings: someone pinging the minimap brings it up so you can see where.
  • Keep open: indoors, while tracking a quest (or only for a while after progress), while tracking creatures or fish, or while tracking herbs, minerals or treasure, except in cities and battlegrounds if you like.
  • Hide in camera modes, camera by camera. On retail, Always show the quest waypoint.
Quest tracker
  • Reveal on a new quest, on objective progress, or on scenario progress, optionally showing just that quest.
  • Stay up for the Calm Timer after a fight, with the tracker ticked In combat.
  • Keep the quest timer up while a timed quest runs (not on retail), and the durability figure while your gear is damaged.
Advanced Frames

Other addons' frames, with the same columns as Standard Frames.

  • Supported addons, handled out of the box when installed: Bartender4, Cooldown Manager Centered, Deadly Boss Mods, Details!, EllesmereUI, ElvUI, FlareUI, Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager, Horizon Suite, Naowh Forever, Plater, Questie, RestedXP Guides and WeakAuras. Some follow the rows of the frames they replace. Click an addon to show or hide its parts.
  • Custom frames: hover over any frame and type /cine add, or press Pick frames by hovering to collect several.
  • Custom Visibility Rules, at the bottom: give any frame, nameplate row or unit frame part a rule of its own.

Questions

Good to know

A frame keeps coming back when I don't expect it.
Something other than its row may be bringing it up: hovering it (and the mouseover group it's in), or one of its page's reveals. Check its page, and the Mouseover groups page.
I turned off a page and the frame still fades.
That's expected. A page's Enable page switch only turns off its extras; the frame still follows its Standard Frames row. Turn off Standard Frames' own switch to leave every frame alone.
Where are the nameplates?
On the Nameplates page, with a guide of their own.
