Cinematic · the cameras

A camera for every moment

Take a flight, sit by a campfire, stare out at a view or step away from the keyboard, and the camera comes alive. Each moment has its own camera, and they hand over to each other smoothly.

How they work

The camera takes over, then hands it back

While CineMode is on, something you do starts a camera: an emote, a cast, a flight, standing still. The camera then moves slowly on its own: it sways, sweeps round you, or drifts in and out. Moving by hand cancels any of them, and everything goes back to normal when the UI returns. Dragging the camera yourself pauses it for a moment, then it carries on from your new view.

Which cameras you get to start with depends on your profile: Subtle leaves the camera to you, Balanced uses a few (flights, going AFK, your Hearthstone and teleports), and Cinema uses every one. The Camera area in Customize changes this in one go, and the profiles guide has more.

    Choose what starts what

    The Cameras page has a row for each trigger: resting at a campfire, /sit, the Campfire button (not on retail), /sleep, /dance, /kneel, a chair, drawing your weapon, your Hearthstone, a teleport, logging out, /stare, Fishing, going AFK, standing still, a flight, a quest giver, auto-walking, auto-running and dying. Each row shows its camera, and small tags for anything set apart from the usual, like music or where it's turned off. Untick the box at the start of a row to turn it off for now (its settings stay). Click a row to open its settings:

    Camera

    Which camera the trigger starts, or No camera. The emotes can pick the cozy, vista, fish or AFK camera; a flight only the flight camera or none. Beside it, Music: whether that camera starts music as it starts (see Music and sound), and CineMode: only with Start in CineMode off, the camera brings CineMode on while it runs, and it ends with the camera.

    When

    Where it can start: the open world, cities, dungeons, raids, PvP, grouped, or a rule of your own with Edit, and the roles it's for. Anywhere else it doesn't start, and stops if it's running; the UI stays faded with the camera left to you. For flights, Cities means taking off from one. Wait for the Calm Timer, in the Combat dropdown, holds the camera back until the Calm Timer has run out after a fight.

    What it needs

    Some triggers have one more line: the campfire buffs, the words that mark a seat, how long you stand still first (120 seconds to start with, up to 300; going AFK with the AFK camera skips the wait), or how long you auto-walk, auto-run or wait at a quest giver before the camera starts.

    When two triggers happen at once, a Hearthstone or teleport cast comes first, then logging out, going AFK, the latest emote, a drawn weapon, a campfire, and standing still last.

    A screensaver. Untick Start in CineMode on the main page, then tick CineMode on the Stand still and Go AFK rows. You keep the normal UI while you play, and CineMode with the AFK camera takes over once you've been idle a while.
    Camera wheel

    Any camera, any time

    Bind Camera wheel (hold) on the Keybinds page. Hold the key and a ring of camera icons comes up around your cursor: AFK, Cozy, Vista, Fish, Tele and Death. Point at one and let go to start it, or tap the key and click one. Pick a running camera to stop it. A grayed-out icon can't start right now, and none start in a fight.

    Each of those also has a keybind of its own: Start the AFK camera, Start the cozy camera and so on, and Test the death camera to see the Death Cam without dying.

    Tuning

    Camera Settings

    Each camera's own settings are on the Camera Settings page: click a camera's name to open them. Most have the same groups: Rotation (how it sways or sweeps), Zoom, Height and Depth of field. Each camera has its own Reset, and a Test button that runs it for 30 seconds where you stand.

    1. Indoors

      Limit the camera indoors: inside buildings and caves the cameras zoom out less and swing less, so the camera doesn't keep pushing into walls and ceilings. You set how far they may zoom out and swing, and No AFK camera sweep indoors keeps the AFK camera from sweeping right round you in tight rooms.

    2. Depth of field

      Each camera's Edge haze slider adds a soft haze around the edges of the screen, as if the camera had focused on you. It needs Depth of field on the Visual Effects page. The Tele Cam uses the Cozy Cam's.

    To keep the whole normal UI somewhere instead, use Turn off in on the main page: see CineMode and fading.

    Commands

    Camera commands

    Type these in the chat box.

    /cine behindSwing the camera back behind your character
    /cine flybyTurn the camera slowly round to look back, then back behind you (again to stop)
    /cine walkDoes the addon think you're walking or auto-running?
    /cine walk flipFix it if it has walk and run backwards
    Questions

    Good to know

    I zoomed in during a camera. Will it fight me?
    No. Zooming or dragging pauses the camera, and it carries on from your new view after a moment. Your own zoom distance comes back once the camera ends.
    The game shows a warning about experimental camera settings.
    That's the Shoulder Cam, which uses one of the game's experimental camera settings. Accept keeps it working, or turn the Shoulder Cam off.
    Does the Quest Cam need CineMode?
    No, it works whether or not CineMode is on. Not while mounted leaves the camera alone when you talk from your mount.
    My RP Walk camera doesn't start.
    Walking is recognized from your speed. Type /cine walk while auto-walking to see what the addon thinks, and /cine walk flip if it has walk and run backwards.
