Cinematic · CineMode and fading

The UI steps back, the world steps forward

CineMode fades your UI between fights and slides the letterbox in. The moment you need something, it comes back: a fight, a window, a look with the mouse.

The basics

What fades, and what brings it back

With CineMode on, the game's frames fade away: action bars, unit frames, the minimap, chat, buffs, the quest tracker and the rest. What's left is the world, with the letterbox bars and the tint over it. A fight brings back the frames you've picked for fights, windows you open (your bags, the spellbook, a vendor) show over it as usual, and typing just shows the chat.

Every frame has its own row on the Standard Frames page. Everything there is hidden in CineMode unless you tick it: Always shows a frame the whole time, the fight columns only while you fight or have something targeted, and the places (the open world, cities, inns, dungeons and so on) the whole time you're there. Frames from other addons are on the Advanced Frames page.

How fast things go and come back is on the CineMode page: the Fade in time and Fade out time (1.5 seconds each to start with), and a much quicker Combat fade in time so your UI is up the instant a fight starts.

Want CineMode off for a while? Untick Start in CineMode on Cinematic's main page. The UI then stays as it is and the camera's left to you, with just the visual effects over it.

What shows right now

Pick a moment to see what CineMode does with your screen. The switch on top is the Combat box under Turn off in on the main page; the profiles leave it unticked.

CineMode

    Smart reveals

    Just what you need, just when you need it

    Faded frames don't have to stay gone. Hover over anything to see it, and a lot of the UI comes up on its own when it has something to tell you:

    Hover

    Point at a faded frame and it fades in. Hovering one frame can bring up its whole group (see Mouseover groups below).

    Chat

    Chat pops up when a message arrives in a window you can see. The Chat page picks which message types and channels count.

    Portrait

    Your portrait shows while you're recovering after a fight.

    Buffs and debuffs

    Gaining or refreshing a buff briefly shows your buffs and debuffs (not on flights). The Buffs/debuffs page can limit it to after combat.

    Durability

    The durability figure shows while your gear is damaged, and can hide in the camera modes. It's on the Quest tracker page.

    Quest tracker

    The tracker comes up as you make progress or pick up a quest.

    Minimap

    The minimap stays up while you're tracking herbs, minerals, treasure or fish.

    Hunter pet

    On Classic clients, a hunter's pet frame stays up while the pet isn't happy.

    In the camera modes these hovers and peeks use their short default times, so they clear the view quickly.

    Mouseover groups

    Point at one, see them all

    On the Mouseover groups page, frames in the same group come up together when you mouse over any of them: point at one action bar and the bars, menus and XP bar all come up, say. A frame can be in more than one group, and one in no group comes up on its own. Click a group's icon to go to it, or the + to make one of your own.

    Stays after mouseover is how long a frame lingers once your mouse leaves it and its group, fade included.

    Fights

    Staying cinematic in fights

    CineMode stays on through fights, and shows the frames you've picked for them on the Standard Frames page. That's how the profiles differ: Balanced brings your whole UI back for a fight, while Cinema keeps almost all of it away. Each row has its own fight columns:

    In combat

    Shows while you fight. With In combat rows stay for the Calm Timer on, it stays up after the fight until the Calm Timer runs out.

    Enemy targeted

    Shows while you have an enemy targeted. Targeting an enemy only ever shows the frames chosen for it.

    Friend targeted

    Shows while you have a friendly player or NPC targeted.

    Cast fade into combat brings up your In combat frames as you cast at an enemy out of combat, as if the fight had already started: they fade in over the cast and are fully up as it lands.

    To switch CineMode off for every fight instead, tick Combat under Turn off in on the main page: being in combat then brings the whole UI back.

    The letterbox and tint can each stay up or step aside during fights, on the Visual Effects page. Party and raid frames fade part by part (role icons, names, the threat glow), and the cast bar can stay hidden outside fights.

    After the fight

    The Calm Timer

    The Calm Timer on the CineMode page is how long things take to settle back into CineMode after a fight. It starts at 60 seconds. While it runs:

    1. Fight frames stay up

      Nameplates and names ticked In combat stay, so they don't drop away between pulls.

    2. Effects wait

      Visual effects that step aside for fights wait before fading back in, and music muted for fights comes back after it.

    3. Cameras wait

      The RP walk, auto-run and cozy cameras wait for it before starting.

    4. Then it all fades out

      Once the timer's over, things fade out at the Fade out time. 0 settles at once.

    Exit Calm Timer when Healthy ends it early once your health and power are back to full: mana, energy and focus refilled, or rage, runic power and the like drained. Its seconds are how long things stay up once you're full. Include your pet waits for your pet's health too.

    Places

    Turning itself off where you'd rather have the full UI

    The Turn off in boxes on Cinematic's main page keep the normal UI in the places you tick:

    Open world

    Anywhere outside cities, dungeons and raids (inns too), while you're not PvP flagged. Flights still go cinematic.

    Cities

    Capital cities, including Dalaran. Flights leaving a city still go cinematic.

    Dungeons and raids

    Keep the normal UI in dungeons and scenarios, and in raids.

    PvP

    While you're flagged for PvP, and in battlegrounds and arenas.

    Party or raid group

    While you're in a party (but not a raid group), or in a raid group wherever you are.

    Moving

    Walking or riding about brings the UI back, say to look for an NPC in a city. Keep VFX on leaves the letterbox and tint up meanwhile. Auto-run, RP walking and flights stay in CineMode.

    CineMode comes back once you've stood still, or kept moving, for the Back after time. Each camera can also be turned off by place, on the Camera Modes page: see The cameras.

    Other addons

    Add mode: fade anything

    Details!, Bartender4, Plater, EllesmereUI, FlareUI, the Cooldown Manager and Forever Enhanced Cooldown Manager are handled out of the box. For anything else, pick the frames yourself:

    1. One frame

      Hover over it and type /cine add. Or type /cine add and its frame name.

    2. Several at once

      Type /cine add mode (or press Pick frames by hovering on the Advanced Frames page) and hover over each one. A gold box marks each frame you pick. Click Stop, or type /cine add mode again, to fade them all. /cine add cancel drops them.

    3. Set when they show

      Added frames get a row on the Advanced Frames page with the same columns as the Standard Frames page. The X takes a frame off the list.

    The game's own frames and ones that already fade are left out. To keep a frame up all the time instead, hover over it and type /cine keep.

    Screenshots

    Hide the UI, keep the look

    The game's Alt+Z hides everything, letterbox and tint included. Cinematic's Hide the UI (keep the look) keybind hides the UI the same way but leaves the tint, letterbox and other effects on screen. It's made for screenshots. Set it on the Keybinds page, next to Toggle CineMode and Toggle visual effects.

    Commands

    Handy commands

    Type these in the chat box.

    /cine add modePick up every frame you hover over, then again to fade them all
    /cine keepAlways show the frame under your mouse
    /cine listShow the extra faded frames
    /cine configOpens Cinematic's settings
    Questions

    Good to know

    A window opened but the rest of my UI stayed hidden. Is that right?
    Yes. Windows like your bags or the spellbook show over CineMode on their own; the faded frames stay faded until you hover them or a fight starts.
    Where do I set when nameplates and names show?
    On the Nameplates page, per kind (mobs, NPCs, your faction, the other faction, pets, totems) and per place. Outside CineMode, nameplates are left to the game.
    Can I hide the floating XP and damage numbers?
    Hide combat text out of combat on the CineMode page hides the numbers in CineMode until a fight starts. Hide all floating text hides XP, damage and healing always; it needs a full game restart.
    Is any of this different on retail?
    The ideas are the same. Some rows exist on only one game (the swing timer and a hunter's pet happiness on Classic clients, the Cooldown Manager where your game has it), and each game keeps its own settings.
