Cinematic · music and sound

The score comes in with the camera

As a camera starts, the music fades in. When the UI comes back, it fades out again. And whatever happens, your own sound settings are put back afterwards.

How it works

Music that follows the cameras

Many players keep the game's music switched off. With the Audio page on, a camera starting switches the music on and fades it in, at your own music volume. It fades out when the UI returns, and your music is switched off again, logging out too.

With your game music on, Cinematic leaves it alone, except for the places and times you tell it to stop (below). Turn the Audio page off and CineMode leaves your music alone altogether.

Music fade time sets how long the music takes to fade in or out: 3 seconds to start with.

Always put back. Your own sound settings are saved first and put back afterwards, even after a crash.
Which cameras

Pick the cameras that start music

Each row on the Cameras page has a Music box: tick it and that row's camera starts music as it starts. Music already playing just carries on. Before you change anything, the flight camera and the vista camera (/sit and /stare) start music, and the rest don't.

Your profile's Sound choice in Customize can change this: the Subtle profile plays no music in CineMode, for one. See the profiles guide.

While you're away

No music while you're AFK

Going AFK or standing still doesn't start music, unless you'd rather it did: tick Go AFK or Stand still in the Music column. Left unticked, they also keep music from coming back after a pause while you're away or standing still, so you don't come back to the keyboard to a song you didn't ask for.

Spacing it out

Music fatigue

Hearing the music swell every few minutes can wear thin. Music fatigue on the Audio page keeps it from starting again too soon: once a camera has started music, no camera starts it again for this long, counted from when it started.

  1. The first one always plays

    The first camera after a login or /reload can always start music.

  2. Coming back doesn't count

    Music coming back after a mute (a fight, a city) isn't a new start.

  3. 0 turns it off

    That's where it starts. The Spaced out choice in Customize's Sound area sets half a minute.

Pausing and muting

When the music steps aside

Leaving a camera mode

Off to start with. Ticked, music fades out once you leave the camera modes and move on: walking off from the cozy camera, say. Going from one camera straight to another (vista to cozy) keeps it playing, as does standing still. A fresh track fades in when the next camera starts.

Music plays in

A row on the Audio page that works like the frame tables: Always, or only out of combat, in a camera mode, or in the places you tick, such as cities, inns, dungeons, raids, or battlegrounds and arenas. Anywhere else it fades out, your own game music too, and back in after.

Out of combat

Music comes back once the Calm Timer has run out after a fight (see CineMode and fading).

One more song

The Death Cam's song

While the Death Cam runs, a song picked at random from its list plays in place of the zone music, and stops when you release or come back. With CineMode music off, it plays only if your game music is on. You can change the songs under Death Cam on the Camera Settings page: they're music file IDs, which Wowhead lists on each sound's page.

Questions

Good to know

Is there a quick way to switch music off?
Left-click Cinematic's minimap button: it has quick switches for music, tint strength and combat.
I keep my game music on. Will Cinematic change it?
Only to fade it out where the Music plays in row says it shouldn't play, and back in after.
Does Cinematic touch my sound effects or voice volume?
No. It only switches the music on and off and fades its volume, and both are saved first and put back, even after a crash.
