Cinematic · how profiles work

Pick a level, then make it yours

Cinematic sets itself up from one choice: how much of the cinematic feel you want. You can then change any part of it, one area at a time, without touching the rest.

Step one

Three levels

The first time Cinematic runs, the welcome window asks you to pick one. Picking one tries it straight away, so you can see it in the game before you close the window. Nothing runs until you pick.

Subtle

A softer look. Your UI and camera stay yours.

A color tint, a vignette and weather over your normal UI. No camera moves on its own and nothing hides, but every camera, fade and the rest of Cinematic are there in Customize and Settings whenever you want them.

Balanced

Clears your screen between fights.

The clutter clears between fights: your UI fades away for the letterbox and tint, and comes straight back for a fight, a window or a look with the mouse. The camera takes over now and then: on flights, when you go AFK, and for your Hearthstone and teleports.

Cinema

Full immersion: just you and the world.

The game's UI stays away even in fights, and Cinematic shows what you need its own way: your health at the screen's edges, an arrow over your target, your resources and the Cooldown Manager in fights. The letterbox stays up, and the camera comes alive on flights, by campfires, at vistas and more.

Step two

Customize, one area at a time

Press Customize in the welcome window to change one part of your level. Your setup is split into seven areas, and each asks one question: picking an answer changes only that area. Everything else stays as your level set it, and each change shows in the game straight away. An eighth page, Visions, turns each vision on or off.

Pick an area below to see its choices, in the order Customize lists them. A gold tag marks the choice a level uses. Some choices exist on only one version of the game, so pick yours.

Customize

    Your changes

    Where your choices are kept

    1. Your first change makes a profile of your own

      The levels stay as they are. Changing an area copies your level into a profile called My setup, and the change goes there.

    2. Later changes go into the same profile

      Keep changing areas and they all collect in My setup. If you've changed settings on the options pages yourself, Customize tells you when an area is a mix of its own.

    3. Go back any time

      Pick a level again in the welcome window, or on the Profiles page in Cinematic's settings, to start over from it.

    Commands

    Getting back to it

    Type these in the chat box.

    /cine welcomeOpens the welcome window to pick a level or Customize
    /cine profileLists the profiles. Add a name to switch to one, like /cine profile Cinema
    /cine resetPuts your current profile back to how it started
    /cine configOpens Cinematic's settings
    Questions

    Good to know

    Will it move my action bars and menus?
    Only through the Layout area. Pick Your own there and Cinematic leaves your Edit Mode layout exactly as it is.
    Can I change a single setting instead?
    Yes. Press Settings in the welcome window, or open Cinematic in the game's AddOns options. Every page there works as before, and your changes go into your own profile.
    How do I stop Cinematic for now?
    Press Turn off in the welcome window. It stays off until you pick a level or press Turn on.
    I used Default, CDM, Lite or another older profile. What happened to it?
    Older profiles that are no longer offered (Default, CDM, Retail, Forever, Camera Only and Solo Explorer) became a profile of your own at login, with your settings as they were. Three were only renamed: VFX Only is now Subtle, Lite is now Balanced, and Pure Cinema is now Cinema.
