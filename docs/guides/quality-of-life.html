Cinematic · Quality of life

Small helpers for life on the road

Sit by a fire with one click, keep casting your line without hunting for the button, and plan your talents before you spend them. These live on the Quality of life pages in Cinematic's settings.

By the fire

The campfire button

WoW ForeverNot on retail

Hover a campfire with the cursor near the middle of the screen, and a campfire icon comes up right where your cursor is. Click it to settle down by the fire, with no emote to type. Move the cursor off it and it goes away.

Campfire button on the General page. Tick Sit, Kneel and Lie down to choose what a click does; with more than one ticked, it's one of them at random each time.

It doesn't show in a fight or while you're mounted. Settling down by the fire has a camera of its own: it's the campfire row on the Cameras page, which starts the Cozy camera.

Stand up with Camp Benefits

Sitting by a Welcoming Campfire for its minute gives you Camp Benefits. With Stand up once you get Camp Benefits ticked, you stand up again as the buff arrives, ready to go.

At the water

Fishing casts

Every game version

The Fishing page handles casting and catching, with the mouse or a gamepad, in CineMode or not. The fish camera itself is on the Cameras page.

Right-click to cast againWhile the fish camera is on and no bobber is out, right-clicking in the world casts Fishing again. While the bobber is out, right-click clicks it as usual. In a fight, with the loot window open or with the mouse on a player or NPC, right-click is left alone.
Also the first cast, with a pole equippedWith a fishing pole in your main hand, standing still, right-clicking in the world casts Fishing, so you don't have to start the first cast yourself. Swap the pole out to have right-click back everywhere.
Wait after a cast endsA short wait after a catch (0.3 seconds to start with), so the click that loots the fish doesn't cast straight away.
Pause after a missed castWhen a cast doesn't land in fishable water, right-click goes back to normal for a while (30 seconds to start with). Move and stand still for a second to end it early.
Hide the Fishing cast barYour cast bar stays hidden while it shows Fishing. Every other spell shows it as usual.
Gamepad: a button casts tooIn gamepad mode, a button of your choice casts whenever right-click would: the left face button (X on an Xbox pad) to start with. See Gamepad.

Every cast still comes from your own click or press: Cinematic never fishes on its own.

For hunters

Ammo that behaves

WoW Forever and ClassicNot on retail, which has no ammo

The Hunter page has two helpers for arrows and bullets.

Smallest ammo stack first after Clean Up BagsClean Up Bags leaves the part stack last, but you shoot from the first, so a full one would run down and leave you two part stacks. Once the bags are cleaned up, the smallest stack is moved first, to be shot first.
Fill quiver button at vendorsArrows and bullets at a vendor get a Fill button that buys enough to fill your quiver or ammo pouch, as far as your money goes. Point at it to see how many and the cost first.
Planning a build

The talent calculator

WoW ForeverNot on retail

With Talent calculator button on the talents window ticked, a button on your talents window opens a planner that looks just like it: your class's three trees side by side, starting from the talents you have.

  1. Left-click a talent to add a point, right-click to take one back. Above the trees: the level the build needs and the points left. Reset and Undo are there too.
  2. Nothing is learned while you plan. When you're happy, Copy over changes puts what the plan adds into your talents window.
  3. Apply them there, as you would by hand. A button on the calculator takes you back to the talents window.

Talents button when inspecting someone: inspecting a player, a button by their level and class opens their talents, and one with their portrait on the talents window takes you back. Neither works in a fight.

/cine talentsOpens the talent calculator
Also on the General page

A few more

Open the character window when dragging gear out of your bagsDrag something you can wear over the game world and the character window opens, ready to drop it on. Off to start with on game versions where the window opened this way makes harmless errors show in error catchers such as BugSack.
Open windows in the middle of the screenWindows the game opens on the left (quest log, group finder...) open on the right instead, near the top, and narrow ones (character, merchant, mail) a little in from the left edge, leaving your character and the quest giver in view.
Drag windows to move themDrag the game's windows by their title bar; each opens where you last dropped it. Put windows back forgets those spots.
Copy chat buttonA small icon in a chat window's corner opens its lines to select and copy with Ctrl+C.
Reset the zoom on login and /reloadOff to start with, on in the Cinema profile. The camera zooms to the distance you set, instead of staying where you left it.

Each Quality of life page has an Enable Page switch at the top: off, none of its helpers work, and each keeps its tick for when you turn it back on.

Questions

Good to know

The campfire button doesn't show.
Keep the cursor near the middle of the screen as you hover the fire, stand still, and make sure you're out of a fight and not mounted.
Right-click casts Fishing when I didn't want it to.
That's the first-cast option, with a pole in your main hand. Swap the pole out, or untick Also the first cast, with a pole equipped.
Does the talent calculator change my talents?
No. It only plans. Copy over changes fills in your talents window, and you still press the game's own Apply.
