Cinematic · Chat and quests

Hear the world without the windows

Chat fades with the rest of the UI and peeks back in when something worth reading arrives. People's words float over their heads, and quest givers talk to you in speech bubbles instead of a window.

The Chat page

Chat that peeks in

In CineMode the chat window fades with the rest of the UI. Putting the mouse over it, or typing, always brings it back. With Show chat when a message arrives (on to start with), a new message briefly shows just the chat window it arrived in, for 8 seconds or as long as you set in Show chat for.

The Chat bubbles page

Chat bubbles

Choose where bubbles show and how they look. The page starts switched off, leaving the game's own bubble settings as you had them; turn it on and they're Cinematic's to set, and yours come back when you turn it off.

Show inA row each for Say & yell (NPCs' too) and Party bubbles: Always, or only in fights, with a target, or in certain places. Nothing ticked: never.
Text only (no background or border)Just the words, floating over the speaker, with a Black outline round the text: Thin, Thick or Thick + shadow.
Text sizeFrom 50% to 200%. Bubbles already up change with the next thing said.

While the page is on, a bubble that would sit at the very top of the screen is moved down below the letterbox bar, or 10% from the top when the bars are off, so you can read all of it.

On retail, the game doesn't let addons change bubbles in instances, so Text only, the text size and keeping bubbles off the top don't apply there.

Speech from behind you

Words from out of view

Someone behind you says something, and there's no bubble on your screen to read it in. Tick Show speech from behind you at the bottom in the Chat bubbles page's Behind you section (it works with that page off) and their words show low in the middle of the screen, with their name, instead of bringing up chat.

Quest Vision · Talking to NPCs

Quest givers who talk to you

With Custom dialogue frames (on to start with), what an NPC says shows in a speech bubble over their head, and what you can say back in a list near the bottom of the screen, instead of the game's gossip and quest windows. The list has everything the window would: quests to turn in, pick up or still in progress, and every other option (vendor, trainer, inn, flight master...).

The quest card

A quest offered or turned in shows its objective and rewards on a card, with its buttons (Accept, Decline, Continue, Complete Quest). Click a reward to choose it. The game's own popups, such as a cost to confirm or a party member's escort quest, come up as usual.

Number keys and Space1 to 9 and 0 pick from the list. Space shows the next paragraph, then does the card's first button. Only keys that do something there are held back from your action bars, and none in a fight.

Questions

Good to know

Chat keeps popping up for things I don't care about.
Untick those kinds on the Chat page, or untick a channel by name. Your own messages and speech you can already see in a bubble don't count, as long as their ticks are on.
An addon expects the game's quest window.
The game's windows stay open while you talk, just out of sight, so other addons still see a conversation. If you'd like to read a quest the usual way, tick the "Show the game's window" link.
The number keys didn't pick a reply.
They don't work in a fight, where the game doesn't let addons borrow keys. Click the reply instead.
