Cinematic · Flight Cam

Every flight a film

Take a flight path and the camera swings behind you, sways through the journey, now and then turns round to take in the view behind you, and settles behind you just before you land.

The journey

From takeoff to landing

  1. Takeoff

    With Start CineMode as soon as you take off, the UI fades and the camera starts straight away, with no wait. Swing round behind you at takeoff slowly brings the camera round behind your character, ready for the sway. Drop your target at takeoff fades your target's frames as if nothing were targeted, until you pick a new target or land. (Addons can't clear the target itself.)

  2. The sway

    Sway behind you swings the camera from side to side behind you, pausing between moves, and drifting slowly through the pauses. It can tilt and zoom slowly as it goes. All of this is under Flight Cam on the Camera Settings page.

  3. Fly-bys

    Now and then in the middle of the flight, the camera slowly turns round to look back past you, holds there, then turns back behind you. More below.

  4. Landing

    Lock the camera behind you before landing: shortly before you land, the sway stops and the camera turns back behind you and stays there until you touch down. Put the zoom back when you land glides the camera back to the zoom you had at takeoff.

Drag the camera, zoom or use a camera key on a flight and it's yours for the rest of that flight: the camera waits a while (15 seconds to start with), then just sways from where you left it. No more fly-bys, no swing back behind you, no slow zoom, no lock behind you before landing and no zoom back when you land: it stays how you set it. /cine flyby still works when you ask.

    Fly-bys

    Looking back at the view

    With Random fly-bys on, the camera lines up behind you, then turns round (160° to start with, 180 looks straight back at you), holds the view for a few seconds, and turns back behind you, easing in and out.

    About one every

    Fly-bys are spread over the middle of the flight: one per minute of it to start with, and at least one, each at a random moment in its share.

    Not before, not after

    They keep away from takeoff and landing: between 25% and 75% of the way, to start with.

    The turn

    Turn round to, Turn round takes, Look back for and Turn back takes set how far and how slowly.

    Do /look

    Off to start with. Tick Do /look as a fly-by starts and your character looks around as the camera starts turning, as if they'd spotted something. Players nearby see the emote in chat.

    Want one now? /cine flyby starts one whenever you like (again to stop), on a flight or standing anywhere, though not in a fight or while you're holding a mouse button down. On a flight it's skipped too close to landing.

    Route timing

    It learns how long each flight takes

    The game doesn't tell addons how long a flight will take, so Cinematic times each route itself, from takeoff to landing, and remembers it. The next time you fly that route, it knows when you'll land: that's what lets it spread the fly-bys over the flight and settle the camera behind you before you touch down.

    On a route's first flight, there's one fly-by early on (30 seconds to a minute after takeoff) and no settling before landing.

    Frequent Flier. On WoW Forever, the Frequent Flier legacy talent makes flight path mounts fly 20% faster. Cinematic checks for the talent itself, so the first flight after you learn it is already timed right, on every route you know.

    If every flight speeds up or slows down for some other reason, one flight on a route it already knows is enough: Cinematic notices that it took clearly longer or shorter, and corrects every route's time at once. One odd flight is put right by the next.

    Questions

    Good to know

    I reloaded mid-flight. Why no fly-bys?
    Random fly-bys are planned as you take off, so a flight picked up after a /reload or login gets none. The sway and the landing still work.
    Can I keep the normal UI in cities but still get the flight camera?
    Yes. Cities under Turn off in on the main page keeps flights leaving a city cinematic. On the Camera Modes page, the Flight Cam's own row decides where it runs: there, Cities means taking off from one.
    Does music play on flights?
    The flight camera is one of the cameras that start music, if the Audio page is on. Untick it in the Music column of the Camera Triggers page. See Music and sound.
    Can I turn the flight camera off altogether?
    Set Take a flight to No camera on the Camera Triggers page, and the camera is left to you for the whole flight.
