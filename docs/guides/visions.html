Cinematic · Visions

See only what matters right now

With the UI faded, a vision brings back just the one thing you need: who needs healing, what your quest wants, who's winning a duel or a roll, and how hurt you are.

At a glance

Five visions

Each vision has a page in the Visions group of Cinematic's settings (Duel Vision is on its General page), with an Enable page switch at the top. Turning a page off keeps all its ticks for when you turn it back on.

For healers

Heal Vision

The game's own party and raid frames show only who needs healing: everyone (pets too) while they're below full health, and tanks as you set them. The group titles hide. Members stay in their places, so there are gaps where the healthy ones are. Out of a fight, putting the mouse over the frames brings everyone back.

Party

Click a member to heal them. With the page's starting values, the tank always shows, the healer and damage dealers show once they're hurt, and the pet only below 35%.

Setting it up

Heal Vision does nothing until you say where. Its page has a row like the other grids in Cinematic:

Show inAlways (everywhere), or the places: Open World, Cities, Dungeons, Raids, BGs & Arenas. Party and Raid turn it on while you're in one, wherever you are.
Combat and targetsIn combat, the Calm Timer after a fight, Enemy targeted and Friend targeted turn it on at those times only.
RolesTo start with it's on only while you're a healer (your specialization's role, or the one you picked in the group finder). Tick tanks or damage too, or none for every role.
Tanks, Healers, Damage, PetsFor each kind, Show below health (100% shows tanks always, at full health too) and Opacity while hidden (0% hides them completely).
Target min opacityThe member you've targeted shows at least this much. 100% always shows them.
Dead, offline or far awayThese are hidden whatever their health, unless one is your target. Far away means more than 100 yards off, or not in your dungeon or raid.

Members with debuffs can't be shown just for their debuffs: the game doesn't let addons see your group's debuffs in fights. In battlegrounds on WoW Forever, which won't say how far anyone is, members out of range of your heals (about 40 yards) fade instead.

For questing

Quest Vision

Mobs and NPCs you still need for a quest objective keep their nameplates up while the rest fade, with a quest mark on them and on your target frame. Once the objective is done, they fade with the rest. Its nameplate parts need the Nameplate & Names page enabled.

Always show theirTick any of Nameplate (the whole plate), Name (the name), Quest mark (!) (a golden ! on whatever shows, or the ! alone; ticked to start with), in CineMode, in fights and out; and Unit frame (the same mark by your target frame's health bar while you target one of them, in CineMode and out; ticked to start with). None ticked: No action.
Quest icon by their nameplates or namesA golden exclamation mark beside their health bars, wherever their plates show.
Quest item buttonA big button with the item your nearest tracked quest gives you to use, where your extra ability button goes. Click it, or press its key (Use the quest item on the Keybinds page). It can't appear or move in a fight: it stays as it was until the fight ends.
Glow on quest objectsThe thing on the ground you pick up or use for an objective glows gold, with the quest mark over it: one at a time, the one in front of you that your interact key would use, up to 20 yards away. To find it, it turns on the game's soft targeting for things you can interact with while a quest in the zone needs it; your own settings come back after.

Quest Vision's page also has Talking to NPCs: the speech bubble and reply list that replace the game's quest windows. That has a guide of its own in Chat and quests.

For groups

Roll Vision

In a group, items being rolled on show in a window, not only as lines in chat: each item, a bar for the time left, and everyone's pick (need, greed, transmog) and number, with the winner lit at the top. It opens as an item drops, just under the game's roll frame, so you can see who's picked what before you choose. Each item fades a few seconds after it's won; point at them to keep them.

Show rolls onUncommon and up, Rare and up (where it starts) or Epic and up. Rolls on the rest stay in chat.
Only items you can useSkip armor or weapons you can't wear, another class's, or a level you haven't reached.
Items shown at onceUp to 8, newest at the top: four down, the rest in a second column.
Players shown per item in a raidThe winner first, then need before greed, highest numbers first; the rest on an "and N more" line. Click an item's panel to list everyone. In a party everyone is listed.
Always list your own roll · Only show the best pickKeep your own line, and hide greed and transmog once someone needs (they can't win it).

Roll Vision reads the game's loot history, the same list /loot shows. On a game version without one, its page doesn't appear.

For watching

Duel Vision

In CineMode, the nameplates of two players dueling near you show, so you can follow their health. Each duel has its own colored border and class icons, and the winner and loser show when it ends. It works in the open world, out of fights, and needs the Nameplate & Names page enabled.

While it watches, it turns on friendly player nameplates, but keeps everyone's invisible and click-through except the two duelists'. Its tick is under Duel Vision on the Visions General page.

For staying alive

Vitals Vision

Your health at a glance, without the portrait. As it drops, the screen's edges close in, with your theme's art soaking in from them (blood, smoke, a glow in its color), a heartbeat that quickens as you weaken and a flash on big hits. The death screen takes over if you die.

Screen edgesThe edges closing in, with their own Starts and Full, and a weaker Strength out of combat for eating and drinking back up. Its Show in row picks where and when: Always, or only in fights, with a target, or in certain places.
Damage Taken AlertWhat counts as a big hit for the flash: 20% of your health at once, or smaller hits adding up to 30%.
Style hub: Vitals VisionThe look has its own page in the Style hub, with a live preview of the edges closing in (its Test button, top right, runs your health down in hits there and on the screen), and a Strength from 0 to 100% that scales the whole look: at 50% it's as set, lower is lighter, higher is stronger.
ThemeAuto picks your class and spec's (on game versions without specs, the talent tree you've put the most into), or pick any. Each class has a theme for each spec.
ShadowHow far in and how dark the edges come, black or tinted, and the color grade that drains the world as they close in.
ArtTwo layers: Our art, Cinematic's painted edge, and Blizzard art, one of the game's own screen effects (fire, frost, lightning, ripples) cut to an edge and laid over ours. Each has its own opacity; set one to 0 to leave it out. Each theme has a Blizzard effect picked to go with it, or choose the pattern, its edge and whether it keeps its own colors or takes the theme's. Classic has only a few of the game's effects.
Our effectsA Heartbeat that throbs from 60 to 140 beats a minute as you weaken, a Flash on big hits, Sparks (motes, stars, lightning and the drifting haze) and a Ripple wave of the game's art creeping in on each heartbeat.

Some game versions keep your health secret from addons in fights. There the edges only fade in and out with it rather than following every point.

Questions

Good to know

I turned Heal Vision on and nothing changed.
Its grid starts with nothing ticked, and its roles start with healers only. Tick a place (or Always) and make sure you're in a healing spec, or tick your role too.
Will Quest Vision change my game settings?
The glow on quest objects turns on the game's soft targeting for interactable things while you need it, and puts your own settings back after. Untick Glow on quest objects if you'd rather it didn't.
Do visions work outside CineMode?
Some do: Roll Vision, Heal Vision, the quest icons on your target frame and the glow on quest objects work either way. Duel Vision and Quest Vision's nameplates are about what shows while CineMode fades things, so they work in CineMode.
Can I turn one off in a fight?
Yes, with the vision wheel or its Toggle keybind. Turning Heal Vision off brings all the party and raid frames back at once.
