Cinematic · nameplates and names

Only the plates that matter

In CineMode, nameplates and names over heads fade like the rest of the UI. You choose, for each kind of unit and each place, which stay: enemies in a fight, your group in a battleground, nothing at all in a city. Outside CineMode they're left to the game.

The Nameplates page

Grids of kinds and places

The Nameplates page, in the UI Manager, says which nameplates and names stay up in CineMode. Each grid has a row per kind of unit and these columns:

  • AlwaysShown everywhere, in fights and out.
  • In CombatIn fights and for the Calm Timer after. In PvP Combat instead: only while you're PvP flagged.
  • Enemy targetedOut of fights, while you have a living enemy targeted.
  • Friend targetedOut of fights, while you have anything else targeted: friends, NPCs, dead enemies.
  • PlacesPvP (flagged, outside battlegrounds and arenas), BGs & Arenas, Open World (inns included), Cities, Grouped (in a party or raid) and Instances (dungeons, scenarios and raids).

Ticking a box also turns those nameplates on in the game's own options, so they can show at all. Like the frame grids, any row can be turned into a rule of its own, for AND, NOT and more places.

The page's Enable page switch turned off leaves nameplates and names alone, and the game's own options decide, in CineMode and out.

    Friendly Plates in Dungeons and Raids

    In dungeons and raids the game won't let addons fade friendly nameplates, so each kind (players, pets and minions, NPCs) is either all on or all off there: Hidden or Shown, and for players and NPCs also With a friendly target (on while you have one of that kind targeted, out of fights; every plate of that kind shows then, not just your target's). Friendly players' setting covers their pets too, so with pets shown, friendly players' plates show as well. The game won't switch nameplates on or off during a fight: they stay as the fight found them.

    To start with, friendly players and NPCs show with one targeted, and pets never.

    Names on faded plates

    A name, with the plate hidden

    In CineMode, away from dungeons and raids, nameplates stay on but faded, and the game takes its own names away from units that have a plate. So where a name is kept up, Cinematic draws it on the faded plate instead, made to look like the game's names over heads: red for hostile units, yellow for neutral ones, green for friendly NPCs and blue for friendly players (green while they're PvP flagged), with no outline, and smaller further off.

    Beyond nameplate range the game's own names take over, so a name can shift a little in size or place as its unit crosses that range.

    The Target Arrow

    With no target frame on screen, it can be hard to tell what you've targeted. The Target Indicator puts a mark on your target's nameplate, shown even while the rest of the plate is faded or shows just its name. Tick its row in the Cinematic Plate Parts grid to say when; set its look on the Target Indicator page in the Style hub. The Cinema profile turns it on all through CineMode.

    Outside CineMode

    What's left to the game

    Outside CineMode, Cinematic doesn't fade nameplates or names: the game's own nameplate and name options decide, as they would without the addon. A few things still apply:

    • Cinematic Plate Parts (class and role icons, the Target Indicator) show outside CineMode only on rows with Always ticked.
    • The Nameplate distance on the Graphics+ page sets how far away plates show, CineMode or not.
    • Beyond nameplate range, the game draws raid markers over units itself, as it does over your own head, and addons can't hide them.

    Visions add their own plates in CineMode: Quest Vision keeps up the plates of mobs you still need for a quest, with a golden !, and Duel Vision shows the two players dueling near you. The visions guide has more.

    Questions

    Good to know

    I hid friendly plates, but in a dungeon they're back.
    In dungeons and raids, friendly plates follow Friendly Plates in Dungeons and Raids instead of the grids: the game doesn't let addons fade them one by one there.
    I changed a setting mid-fight and nothing happened.
    The game only switches nameplates on or off out of combat. Your change applies as the fight ends.
    My target's plate shows even though its kind is unticked.
    That's the Target Nameplate grid, which starts with Always ticked for every kind. Untick a row there to let the main grids decide.
    Can I see plates farther away?
    Yes, with Nameplate distance on the Graphics+ page, up to the game's own limit.
