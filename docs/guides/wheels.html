Cinematic · Wheels

Everything in reach from one key

Hold a key and a ring of icons comes up round your cursor: cameras, the game's menus, raid markers, visions, or commands of your own. Point at one and let go. No bars needed, even with the UI hidden.

Using a wheel

Hold it, or tap it

Every wheel works the same two ways, so pick whichever feels natural.

Hold
  1. Hold the wheel's key. The ring comes up round your cursor.
  2. Point at an icon. Its name shows in the middle.
  3. Let go of the key to pick it.
Tap
  1. Tap the key. The wheel stays up.
  2. Click the icon you want.
  3. Or put it away: click the middle, press Escape or tap the key again.

Changed your mind while holding? Move back to the middle and let go: nothing is picked and the wheel stays up for a click.

Try it

Point at a wheel

Pick a wheel, then point at its icons (or tap them on a phone). Click one to pick it.

Wheels

The wheels

What each one holds

Master wheelOne icon for each of the other wheels: point at one and let go to open it, then click what you want on it. One key for all of them. Choose which wheels sit on it, and in what order, on its page (Wheels on it, with Usual order to start over).
Camera wheelThe cameras you can start by hand: AFK, Cozy, Vista, Fish, Tele and the Death camera. Picking the camera that's running stops it. It can't open in a fight.
Menu wheelThe game's menu buttons, the ones your menu bar has on your game version, in its order: character, spellbook, talents, quest log and the rest. Opens their windows even with the UI hidden, in a fight too. Picking a window that's open closes it.
Marker wheelThe raid target markers (skull, cross, square...) for your target, or with none, whatever the cursor is on. None takes theirs off. In a fight it always marks your target.
Ground marker wheelThe colored lights you drop on the floor: it drops under the cursor where you let go. Picking one that's already down moves it; Clear all takes them all up. Only in a group, on game versions with ground markers.
Vision wheelHeal, Quest, Duel, Roll and Vitals Vision, ringed while they're on: pick one to turn it on or off, in a fight too. See Visions.

The marker, ground marker and vision wheels can hold icons of your own too, after their own (Your icons on it, on each wheel's page). Each runs a command, as on your own wheels.

Your wheels

Wheels of your own

Make up to 8 wheels on the Your wheels page. Each icon on one runs a command when you pick it, such as /rw Pull in 10 or /readycheck: anything a macro can do, a line each, in a fight too. They're the same for all your characters.

  1. New wheel makes an empty one. Give it a Name (shown in its middle, on the master wheel and in the game's key bindings) and an icon.
  2. Add an icon for each command: pick its icon from every icon the game has, give it a name (or leave it empty to show the command's first line) and type its command. Test runs it now.
  3. Give the wheel its own key, or tick On the master wheel.

Share wheel makes a line of text for friends; they add it with Import a wheel, which lists every command first. Read them before you add one: each runs as if you'd typed it, and /run lines can do anything an addon can.

The game cuts a command line at 255 letters, so keep long ones in a macro and run that.

Setting up

Giving a wheel its key

No wheel has a key at first. Open the Wheels group in Cinematic's settings: each wheel's page shows its ring as it is now, with its Key right there, and an On the master wheel tick. The same keys are on the Keybinds page, and in the game's own Key Bindings under AddOns.

A good start is a single key for the master wheel: every other wheel is then one point away.

Questions

Good to know

Why doesn't the camera wheel open in a fight?
Unlike the others, it isn't built from the game's protected buttons, which are the only kind that can open in a fight. Open it again once the fight is over, from its own key or the master wheel. Every other wheel works in fights.
The marker wheel marked the wrong thing.
It marks your target, or with no target, whatever is under the cursor as you press the key. In a fight it's always your target: the game doesn't let addons change who it marks then.
My menu wheel looks different from the one here.
It mirrors your menu bar, so it has the buttons your game version shows, in the same order.
Can I take a wheel off the master wheel?
Yes: untick On the master wheel on its page. Its own key still opens it.
