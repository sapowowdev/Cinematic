Reference · settings

Every settings page, in one line

Cinematic's settings are in the game's Options under AddOns › Cinematic, or type /cine config. Each name below links to the guide that explains it.

Finding your way

Most pages sit inside a group, such as UI Manager or Camera: open the group and pick a page from the row of icons at its top (point at an icon to see its name). Every page or group has a search box for its settings and a Reset page button that puts just that page back to your profile's own values. The main page's search box looks through everything.

Pages are listed here in the order the game shows them. A tag means the page only shows on some versions of the game; see WoW Forever and retail.

At the top
Cinematic
The main page: Enable Cinematic, Start in CineMode, the minimap button, the language, the menu look (HD or SD), where CineMode turns itself off, and a search box for every setting.
Profiles
Pick a profile; make, rename, delete or reset your own, and share them with Export and Import.
CineMode
How fast the UI fades, what brings it (or parts of it) back while CineMode is on, and combat text.
UI Manager
Standard Frames
Which of the game's frames stay up in CineMode: always, in fights, with a target, or in chosen places.
Advanced Frames
Supported addons' frames and frames you added yourself (Add mode), and how they fade.
Nameplates
Which nameplates and names stay up in CineMode: in groups, in fights and in each kind of place.
Unit Frames Some versions
Which parts of your frame, your pet's, and the target, focus and target of target frames show, CineMode or not.
Personal Resources
Your player frame, pet frame and personal resource display: kept up while you recover or run low on mana, held back at the start of a fight.
Mouseover groups
Frames in the same group come up together when you point at any of them. Make groups of your own.
Minimap
When the minimap stays up in CineMode, such as while tracking herbs or minerals.
Quest tracker
When the quest tracker comes up in CineMode, such as when you make progress or pick up a quest.
Swing timer WoW Forever and Classic
Which of the game's swing timer bars show, in CineMode and out.
Cooldown Manager With a Cooldown Manager
When the Cooldown Manager comes up in CineMode, and showing spells only when they're ready.
Tooltips
Hide tooltips in CineMode, in fights or in the camera modes, with a list of the ones that still show. Off by default.
Buffs/debuffs
Your buff and debuff icons fade with the UI; these bring them back, such as when you gain one.
Chat
How the chat window fades, and which messages and channels make it peek in.
Chat bubbles
Where chat bubbles show, and how they look.
Gamepad WoW Forever
The frames gamepad mode adds: its action bars, the button hints and the reticle.
Visions
General
Duel Vision (the health of a duel near you) and the Vision Helper on the minimap button.
Heal Vision
The party and raid frames show only who needs healing.
Quest Vision
Mobs and NPCs you still need for a quest keep their nameplates up with a quest mark, and the quest object in front of you glows.
Roll Vision With a loot history
Loot rolls in a window instead of only in chat: everyone's pick and number, the winner on top.
Vitals Vision
Your health at a glance, without the portrait: the screen's edges close in as it drops.
Quality of life
General
Small helpers for getting about the world, such as the campfire button and the talent calculator on WoW Forever.
Fishing
Casting and catching, with the mouse or a gamepad, CineMode or not.
Hunter WoW Forever and Classic
Smallest ammo stack first after Clean Up Bags, and a Fill quiver button at vendors.
Master wheel
A wheel of the other wheels, and which ones are on it.
Your wheels
Make wheels of your own, each icon running a command, and share or import them.
Camera wheel
The cameras on the camera wheel.
Menu wheel
The game's menu buttons, in reach with the UI hidden.
Marker wheel
Raid target markers, plus icons of your own.
Ground marker wheel Some versions
Ground markers dropped under the cursor, plus icons of your own.
Vision wheel
The visions, turned on and off from a ring, plus icons of your own.
Visual Effects
General
Letterbox bars, vignette, inn glow, weather and depth of field.
Tint
The color grading over the world: a preset, the time of day and each zone's mood.
Graphics+
The game's own graphics settings one by one, past its Graphics menu either way. The same in every profile.
Style
Names
How the names Cinematic draws on faded nameplates look in CineMode: colors (and friendly players' color), outline, opacity, size and height, with presets and a preview.
Target Indicator
How your target, focus and the unit under the mouse stand out on the nameplates: a grid with a row for each (and one for your target while it's attacking you), each with its own mark, color, size and movement, and which of the unit's name, health bar or border take its mark's color.
Audio
Audio
Sound during CineMode: which cameras play music, music fatigue and muting by place. Your own sound settings are put back afterwards.
Camera
Cameras
A row per trigger (an emote, a campfire, going AFK, a flight...): which camera it starts (or none), whether it brings CineMode on and starts music, and where it can start.
Camera Settings
Each camera's own settings, in a section apiece, each with its own Reset. The flight camera's are explained in Flight Cam.
