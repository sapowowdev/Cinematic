Reference · keybinds

Every keybind

None of these has a key until you give it one. Set them on Cinematic's Keybinds settings page, or in the game's Key Bindings under AddOns: they're the same bindings, so either place works.

In the game's Key Bindings each name starts with Cinematic:, as below. The Keybinds settings page lists the same keys in groups, some with shorter names (shown after the dash). Keys marked hold do their thing while held: hold, point and let go, or tap and then click.

General

CineMode and the UI

Toggle CineMode
Turns CineMode on or off, like /cine.
Hide the UI (keep the look) — Hide the UI
Like the game's Alt+Z: hides the whole UI, but keeps the tint, letterbox and time-of-day title. Made for screenshots. Press again (or the game's own key) to bring it back.
Toggle visual effects
Turns the Visual Effects page on or off (its Enable page switch): the letterbox, tint, vignette, weather and the rest.
Wheels

Hold a key, pick from a ring

Master wheel (hold)
A wheel of the wheels below (camera, menu, marker...): point at one and let go to open it, then click what you want on it. Tap it instead and the wheel stays up for you to click one. The camera wheel can't open in a fight.
Camera wheel (hold)
A wheel of the cameras, an icon for each: point at one and let go to start it. Tap it instead and the wheel stays up for you to click one. Picking the camera that's running stops it.
Menu wheel (hold)
A wheel of the game's menu buttons (character, spellbook, quest log...), the ones on your menu bar: point at one and let go to open its window, even with the UI hidden.
Marker wheel (hold)
The raid target markers (skull, cross, square...) for your target, or with none, whatever the cursor is on. Point at one and let go to put it on them, or at None to take theirs off.
Ground marker wheel (hold)
The ground markers, the colored lights you drop on the floor: point at one and let go, and it drops under the cursor. Picking one that's down moves it; Clear all takes them all up. Only in a group, and only on game versions that have ground markers.
Vision wheel (hold)
The visions (Heal, Quest, Duel...), ringed while they're on: point at one and let go to turn it on or off, in a fight too.
Your wheels 1 to 8
Wheels you make yourself on the Wheels settings page, each icon running a command of your choice. Until you make one its key is called Your wheel 1 (not made yet) and so on; after, it takes your wheel's name, like Raid tools (hold). Set their keys on the Wheels settings page or in Key Bindings.

The Wheels guide covers what goes on each wheel.

Visions

Turning visions on and off

Toggle Heal Vision
Turns Heal Vision on or off (its Enable page switch), in a fight too. Off, the party and raid frames all show again.
Toggle Quest Vision
Turns Quest Vision on or off (its Enable page switch).
Use the quest item
Uses the item on Quest Vision's quest item button, while it shows.
Toggle Duel Vision
Turns Duel Vision on or off.
Toggle Roll Vision
Turns Roll Vision on or off. Only on game versions that keep a loot history.
Toggle Vitals Vision
Turns Vitals Vision on or off (its Enable page switch).

What each vision does is in the Visions guide.

Cameras

Starting a camera on demand

Each starts its camera now, as if its event had happened. Press it again, move or jump to stop it.

Start the AFK camera — AFK camera
Starts the AFK camera now, without waiting out its delay.
Start the cozy camera — Cozy camera
Starts the cozy camera now, as if you'd sat down.
Start the vista camera — Vista camera
Starts the vista camera now, as if you'd done a /stare.
Start the fish camera — Fish camera
Starts the fish camera now, without casting.
Start the tele camera — Tele camera
Starts the tele camera now, as if you were casting your Hearthstone.
Test the death camera — Death camera test
Pretends you're dead for 30 seconds so the death camera runs. Press again to stop. The death camera has to be turned on.

Tooltips

Hidden tooltips

Show hidden tooltips (hold) — Show hidden tooltips
Hold to see the tooltips the Tooltips page hides, in combat and in CineMode. Pressing it while you're on a unit or something in the world brings its tooltip up; anything else, point at it again with the key held.
Toggle tooltip hiding
Turns the Tooltips page on or off (its Enable page switch).
