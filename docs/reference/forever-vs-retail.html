Reference · game versions

WoW Forever and retail

Cinematic is one addon for every version of the game. Almost everything works everywhere; a few features belong to one side, because only that game has the part they work with.

Two sides

Cinematic sorts the game versions into two: Retail and everything else, which this guide calls WoW Forever and Classic. It's made first and foremost for WoW Forever, so that's where it's tested most; the other Classic versions get the same features, but please report anything that's off.

Settings that only apply to one game only show up there, and each game keeps its own settings and profiles. You won't see an option your game can't use.

Everywhere

On every version

CineMode and fading, smart reveals and mouseover groups, every camera (flights, AFK, RP walk, auto-run, cozy, tele, vista, fish, quest and death), the tint, time of day, zone moods, inn glow, letterbox and vignette, music, the visions, the wheels, nameplates and names, chat and chat bubbles, tooltips, Graphics+, profiles and sharing.

Not on retail

WoW Forever and Classic only

Swing timer page
The game's swing timer bars fade between fights, with the ranged bar on top for classes that use it.
Hunter page
The smallest ammo stack goes first after Clean Up Bags, and a Fill quiver button at vendors buys enough to fill your quiver or ammo pouch.
Unhappy pet
For hunters: your pet frame stays up while your pet isn't happy.
Campfire button
Point at a campfire and click the icon at your cursor to sit, kneel or lie down by the fire. It has its own row on the Camera Triggers page.
Stand up with Camp Benefits
Once the buff arrives, you stand up again, ready to go.
Talent calculator
A button on your talents window (or /cine talents) opens a planner that looks just like it, starting from your talents.
Talents when inspecting
A button on the inspect window opens the player's talents, and one on the talents window takes you back.
Day/night dial
A row of its own on the Standard Frames page, to keep the minimap's day/night dial up.
Layouts in Customize
The Layout area offers WoW Forever's (the game's own layout) and Like retail (action bars centered along the bottom, menus in the corner).
Gamepad page WoW Forever
WoW Forever's gamepad mode fades with the rest: its action bars, button hints and reticle, each with a row of its own.
Naowh Forever WoW Forever
Its top bar fades in CineMode (a row on the Advanced Frames page), and its RestedXP guide window fades with the quest tracker.
Menu look
On Auto, Cinematic's settings and windows follow your Character Models setting on WoW Forever: HD or SD.
Retail

Retail only

Extra action and zone ability
A row of its own on the Standard Frames page.
Quest waypoint
The Minimap page can keep the quest waypoint up in CineMode too.
Layouts in Customize
The Layout area offers Resources in the middle (your resource display in the middle, the Cooldown Manager above it; Cinema's choice on retail), and the Action bars area offers Retail's own (each on its own, the micro menu up in fights).
Skyriding
The auto-run camera keeps going when you auto-run on a skyriding mount.
Every expansion's zones
Zone moods cover every expansion, from Outland's fel-scorched Hellfire to Midnight's Voidstorm.
Menu look
On Auto, Cinematic's settings and windows use the HD look.

Questions

Good to know

I play both. Do I set Cinematic up twice?
Yes: each game keeps its own settings. To bring your setup across, export it on one and import it on the other. Settings that only apply to one game only do anything there.
Do the three levels differ between games?
A little, where the games differ: mostly the layout and action bar choices above. The Profiles guide lets you switch between WoW Forever and retail to compare.
Something only goes wrong on one version.
Please report it, with /cine log: the log says which game you're on. See Troubleshooting.
